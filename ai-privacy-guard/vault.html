What should I protect?

Fingerprints only

Clotr checks what you type into AI chats like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, and warns you before a password, a key or a personal detail goes out. You decide: remove it, or send it anyway.

  • Only on AI chats. It never runs on your email, your bank or any other website.
  • Nothing leaves this computer. Clotr has no servers and never connects to the internet.
  • It never saves what you type. It keeps a short note of what it found, like “Phone number · chatgpt.com · hidden”, which you can see in its toolbar button.
  • It only warns. Out of the box, a note appears in the corner and your message is yours to send.
  • Only in this browser. It can't see AI apps on your computer or phone (like the ChatGPT app), or AI built into other programs; use those with the same care.
